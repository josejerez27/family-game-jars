Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version: Family Game Jars doesn't collect, send or share any personal information. Everything you type stays on your own device.

This policy covers the Family Game Jars app and website, including Hi Bored, I'm Dad (Bored Jar), Christmas Game Jar and Baby Shower Game Jar.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves a few things only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • The first names (or nicknames) and ages you enter on "Who's playing?"
  • Which ideas or games are in your jar, your party plan and your custom ideas
  • Your trophy list of games you've played, with dates
  • Settings such as filters and team setups

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. We have no server, database or account system, so we couldn't see it even if we wanted to. Each phone keeps its own copy, and nothing syncs between devices.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access
  • No in-app purchases
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone

Device features

The timer can play a beep and make the phone vibrate when time is up. That's all it uses, and neither needs any special permission.

Links to other websites

Some ideas include optional links to outside sites for tutorials or activities, such as YouTube, Scratch or Code.org. Those links open outside the app, and those sites have their own privacy policies. We encourage a grown-up to open these links with kids.

Children

Family Game Jars is made for families to use together, and kids often play it. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We recommend a parent or guardian set up "Who's playing?" Using first names or nicknames is plenty.

Deleting your data

You're always in control. To erase everything the app has saved, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. On the website, clear the site's data in your browser.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, when the site is visited. See GitHub's privacy statement. Once installed, the app works offline.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
